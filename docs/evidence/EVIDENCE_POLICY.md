# Product Media and Evidence Policy

RepairFlow retains no more than 18 final images on the default branch.

Final images must demonstrate product behaviour, architecture, testing, security, reliability, performance or release quality. Uncropped terminal history, duplicate screens, temporary automation captures and decorative mock interfaces are excluded.

The capture pipeline uses fixed seed data, deterministic window sizes and a manifest. Six images are shown directly near the top of `README.md`; the remaining images are placed in a compact evidence section.
