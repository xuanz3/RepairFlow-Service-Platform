# RepairFlow v0.4.0-mobile-preview

## Included

- Expo Router repair queue and case workspace;
- SQLite local workflow persistence in WAL mode;
- validated device check-in;
- QR and serial barcode scanning;
- camera evidence capture with persistent file copy and SHA-256;
- diagnosis, repair action and quality-review flows;
- versioned Maestro workflow contract.

## Limitations

- this preview is not an App Store or Play Store package;
- camera behaviour depends on physical device or simulator capabilities;
- network synchronisation, outbox processing and interrupted upload recovery are Phase 3 work.
