# RepairFlow Product Brief

## Problem

Electronics repair teams often collect condition photographs, diagnostic observations, repair notes and delivery checks across disconnected tools. Mobile staff need fast evidence capture at the counter or workbench, while desktop staff need a structured workspace for triage, comparison, review and reporting. Connectivity interruptions must not lose or duplicate case data.

## Product

RepairFlow is an offline-first repair operations platform for smartphones, laptops, tablets and game consoles. It connects a React Native mobile application, an Electron desktop application and an ASP.NET Core service.

## Primary users

- **Intake Coordinator:** records device condition and authorised service scope.
- **Repair Technician:** performs diagnosis, records work and attaches evidence.
- **Quality Reviewer / Workshop Admin:** resolves conflicts, verifies quality and approves delivery.

## Core outcome

A repair case can move from check-in to delivery with a complete, integrity-checked and auditable record even when a client device is temporarily offline.
