# Scope and Workflows

## v1 scope

- Device check-in with fictional customer aliases
- Before-repair condition evidence and image annotation
- QR-based repair-case lookup
- Diagnostic checklists and results
- Repair actions and limited part-use records
- Quality verification and delivery approval
- Offline-first mobile and desktop clients
- Attachment integrity verification
- Role-based access and audit history
- Cross-platform packaging and automated product media

## Exclusions

- Payments, invoices or accounting
- Full stock purchasing and warehouse management
- Customer marketing or contact campaigns
- Real-time chat or video support
- Device unlocking or password storage
- Customer-file inspection
- Commercial store distribution in v1

## Main workflow

`Checked In → Diagnosing → Awaiting Approval → Repairing → Quality Review → Ready for Delivery → Delivered`

A failed quality review returns the case to `Repairing`. A cancelled case retains its audit and evidence history.
